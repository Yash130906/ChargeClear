# ChargeClear design brief

## Design movement
Premium legal-tech editorial: the clarity and warmth of a modern fintech product with the restraint of a trusted legal publication.

## Core principles
- Make the first useful action obvious.
- Treat uncertainty as a visible product feature, not a failure state.
- Use contrast to separate facts, allegations, sources, and next steps.
- Create an atmosphere of calm competence rather than institutional coldness.

## Color philosophy
Deep ink/navy (`#101820`) anchors the brand. Warm paper (`#F7F5EF`) keeps long-form reading humane. Electric teal (`#21D4C3`) signals progress and verified clarity. Coral (`#FF7666`) is reserved for urgency and escalation. Supporting tones are fog gray, sage, and soft sand.

## Layout paradigm
Editorial landing page with asymmetric hero copy and a product UI rail. Wide, generous sections; rounded-but-not-bubbly cards; subtle hairline borders; modular proof blocks. The demo is a focused workspace with a slim step rail, dense but breathable result cards, and progressive disclosure.

## Signature elements
A small ChargeClear wordmark with a dual-shape mark: two interlocking document corners forming a clear path. Source chips, confidence meters, numbered workflow spine, tiny eyebrow labels, and serif display headlines paired with crisp sans-serif UI copy.

## Interaction philosophy
Every interaction answers one of three questions: what is happening, what do I know, what can I do next? Use optimistic transitions, gentle hover lifts, and clear button states. Upload state progresses through staged analysis; result content reveals in a calm cascade.

## Animation
Fast ease-out reveals (300–500ms), a soft progress shimmer during processing, gentle ambient grain and radial glows, no gratuitous parallax. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography system
Display: `DM Serif Display` for emotionally resonant headlines. UI/body: `Manrope` for readability and modern product density. Uppercase eyebrow labels use Manrope with wide tracking. Body measure stays around 65 characters.

## Brand essence
The navigation layer between receiving a legal document and knowing what information and human help to seek next.

## Brand voice
Plainspoken, precise, warm, careful. Say “This appears to be…” rather than pretending certainty. Explain, don’t impersonate counsel.

## Wordmark / logo
A compact `CC` path mark built from two offset document-corner shapes, paired with the ChargeClear name in Manrope. Keep it flat, high-contrast, and legible at favicon scale.

## Signature brand color
Electric teal `#21D4C3`, used as the trust / verified accent against deep ink.
